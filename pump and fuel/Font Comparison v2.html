REF · GT America Trial
The benchmark. Paid (Grilli Type license needed). Warmest, most distinctive. Worth buying if budget allows.
1 · Space Grotesk
⭐ Closest free alternative to GT America. Has quirky ink-trap details, warm personality. Bold weight is strong without being aggressive. Space Mono pairs beautifully.
2 · Archivo Black
Most muscular and heavy. Newspaper-grade boldness. Works brilliantly for CTAs and workout names at large sizes. Body text at regular weight is clean.
3 · Hanken Grotesk
Most "invisible" quality — extremely readable, warm, but doesn't shout its own personality as loudly. Excellent if you want the app content to lead, not the font.
4 · Barlow Semi Condensed
Most sporty/utilitarian. The semi-condensed width feels purposeful for a workout app. ExtraBold is very punchy. Most legibility at small sizes.
