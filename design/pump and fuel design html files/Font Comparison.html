NOTES ON EACH PAIRING
A · GT America
Most balanced. Warm but structured. The Mono has great tabular figures. Feels premium and editorial. ✦ Best all-rounder.
B · IBM Plex
Most "credible tool" feel. Slightly clinical but authoritative. Tabular figures are excellent. Best choice if Bloomberg DNA is top priority.
C · Instrument Serif
Most human/warm. The italic serif for onboarding questions creates a genuinely unique voice. Mixed serif+mono is a strong editorial statement.
D · MD Nichrome
Most distinctive. Retro-futuristic, unlike any fitness app. PUSH DAY in Nichrome looks incredible. Risky but memorable. Strong for logo/branding.
